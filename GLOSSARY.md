# RescueBeacon

RescueBeacon helps an operator assess flood observations and identify locations that may need assistance.

## Language

**Operator**:
A first responder responsible for reviewing uncertain assessments and deciding whether assistance is needed.

**Drone mission**:
An inspection of a location to gather more evidence about a possible need for assistance.

**Model assessment**:
The model's judgment about an observation, including its confidence and the evidence it identifies.

**Observation**:
An image tied to a geographic location and evaluated for flooding and possible danger.

**Possible danger**:
Evidence of a potentially dangerous situation, such as a vehicle in floodwater, without confirmation that someone needs help.

**Assistance needed**:
A finding that someone at an observation location needs a response, reached through a confident assessment or an operator decision.

**Response notification**:
An alert to a response team that assistance is needed at an observation location.

**Drone base**:
A location from which a drone mission begins.
